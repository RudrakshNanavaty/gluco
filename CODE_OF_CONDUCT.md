# Gluco Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in Gluco welcoming and harassment-free for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that support an open, respectful, diverse, and
healthy community. Because this project concerns health-related research, we
also expect extra care around privacy, uncertainty, and potentially sensitive
personal experiences.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific in reviews and discussions.
- Welcome questions and differing levels of technical experience.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, not the person.
- Credit contributors and sources accurately.
- Protect credentials, participant data, and private conversations.
- Correct misleading medical or scientific claims promptly and calmly.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Trolling, personal attacks, sustained disruption, or publishing another
  person's private information without permission.
- Sharing credentials, health records, identifying research data, or private
  team communications in public issues, demos, or recordings.
- Falsifying test, review, contribution, rehearsal, or research evidence.

## Enforcement

Report a concern privately to the repository maintainers through the contact
method listed on the maintainers' GitHub profiles. Do not include sensitive
details in a public issue. Maintainers will acknowledge reports, review them
fairly, preserve confidentiality where possible, and take proportionate action.

Possible consequences include a private correction, warning, removal of
content, temporary restriction, or permanent exclusion from project spaces.
Maintainers who have a conflict of interest must step aside from the decision.

## Scope

This code applies in repository, review, issue, discussion, chat, meeting, and
demonstration spaces, and whenever someone officially represents Gluco.
